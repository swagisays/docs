[WhatsEra](https://whatsera.com) connects WhatsApp numbers by scanning a QR code. Linking it to AdsEra lets you manage those numbers, campaigns and auto-replies from inside AdsEra.

---

## Adding the integration

<Steps>
<Step title="Generate a token in WhatsEra">
  Log in to WhatsEra, open the **API** section and click **Generate Token**. Copy the token.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect in AdsEra">
  Open **Marketplace → WhatsEra** and click **Connect**. Paste the WhatsEra token and click **Save**. AdsEra fetches your WhatsApp accounts.
</Step>
<Step title="Scan a number">
  On the **Channels** tab, click **New WA Account**, give it a name and scan the QR code with WhatsApp on your phone (**Linked devices → Link a device**).
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Using the integration

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Connections** | See your WhatsEra connection. Click **Change token** to replace the token. |
| **Channels** | Your WhatsApp accounts with their number and status: **Active**, **Disconnected** or **Expired**. |
| **Campaigns** | Start, stop and delete WhatsEra campaigns. |
| **Auto-Reply** | Keyword replies: the keywords, how they match, the replies and their status. Click **New AutoReply** to add one. |

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="A channel shows Disconnected or Expired">
  The phone logged out the linked device or was offline too long. Scan the QR code again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The token is rejected">
  Generate a new token in WhatsEra and use **Change token**.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Make sure you scan with the right phone. Linking a new device can sign out another linked session.
</Warning>

---

## Removing the integration

1. On the **Channels** tab, remove the WhatsApp accounts you no longer want. You can also unlink the device on your phone under **Linked devices**.
2. In WhatsEra, regenerate or delete the API token. AdsEra can then no longer reach your WhatsEra account.

Campaign and auto-reply history already in AdsEra is kept.
