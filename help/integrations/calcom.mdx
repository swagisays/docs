With Cal.com connected, AdsEra:

- **Receives every booking.** A contact is created or updated, and its **Booking Status**, **Start**, **Event** and **Link** fields stay current, so your workflows can send confirmations and reminders.
- **Lets your agents book.** Addy can offer free times and book meetings in WhatsApp chats, and Ask AI can look up, book, reschedule or cancel bookings for your team.

---

## Adding the integration

### Before you begin

- An AdsEra account with access to **Marketplace**.
- A Cal.com account. For **team** (round-robin or collective) event types, use the API key of a team **admin or owner**.
- A free connection on your plan. The page shows how many you have used, for example "1 of 4 connections used".

### Connect

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an API key in Cal.com">
  In Cal.com, open **Settings → Developer → API Keys** and create a key. We recommend **no expiry**, so the connection doesn't stop working later. Copy the key.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste it in AdsEra">
  In AdsEra, open **Marketplace → Cal.com** and click **Connect Cal.com account**. Enter a **Label** (for example "Sales team Cal.com"), paste the **Cal.com API key** and click **Connect**.
</Step>
<Step title="Done">
  AdsEra checks the key and sets up the booking webhook in Cal.com for you. The account appears with the status **active**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Using Cal.com Teams?** Cal.com needs a webhook on each team event type. AdsEra adds them when you connect. After you create a **new** team event type in Cal.com, click **Sync team events** on the connection so its bookings reach AdsEra. You can check this in Cal.com under **Event Types → the team event → Webhooks**.
</Note>

## Using the integration

The Cal.com page has four tabs.

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Connections** | Connect, rename, replace the key, sync team events, choose contact fields, disconnect. |
| **Bookings** | Every booking received, filtered by status, connection and date. Click a booking to see attendees and history, or open the contact. |
| **Used by agents** | Choose what Addy, Ask AI and the Meta AI agent may do with Cal.com. |
| **Activity** | A log of what the integration did. |

### Save booking answers on the contact

Click **Contact fields** on a connection. Each row maps a **booking field or form question** to a **contact attribute**. Click **Save**, or **Reset to default** to go back to the standard mapping. With no rows, bookings don't change contact attributes.

### Let Addy book in WhatsApp chats

Open **Addy → Goals → Book appointments**, switch on **Offer bookings** and choose **Book in the chat (Cal.com)**. Pick the account and the event type, then save. Addy offers free times from your Cal.com calendar and books the one the customer picks.

### What agents can do

Set these on the **Used by agents** tab. Anything that changes a booking starts **off**.

| Group | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Free times | Find free times in your calendar to offer the customer |
| Book meetings | Book the time the customer picked, with their name and email |
| Customer's own meetings | Tell a customer the time and link of the meetings they booked |
| Reschedule | Move a booked meeting to another free time |
| See bookings | See every booking, the event types and the connection status (your team) |
| Cancel bookings | Cancel a booking; Cal.com tells the attendee |

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="The key is rejected when connecting">
  Check that you copied the whole key and that it hasn't expired. Create a new key in **Settings → Developer → API Keys** and try again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Bookings from a team event don't arrive">
  Click **Sync team events** on the connection. If the **Team events** column shows a warning, the key isn't from a team admin or owner. Click **Replace key** and use an admin's key.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The status shows &quot;error&quot;">
  The **Last error** column shows Cal.com's message. Most often the key was deleted or expired. Click **Replace key** and paste a new key from the same Cal.com account.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="&quot;Your plan limit is reached&quot;">
  Disconnect an account you no longer use, or upgrade your AdsEra plan.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Removing the integration

1. Open **Marketplace → Cal.com**.
2. Click **Disconnect** on the connection and confirm.

**What happens:**

- AdsEra removes its webhooks from Cal.com and deletes the connection: the API key, settings and account details.
- AdsEra stops receiving that account's bookings. Addy and Ask AI can no longer book with it.
- **Bookings and contacts already in AdsEra are kept.**
- To use it again, connect it as new.

You can also delete the API key in Cal.com. AdsEra then shows the connection as **error** until you disconnect it or replace the key.
