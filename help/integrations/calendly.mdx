With Calendly connected, AdsEra:

- **Receives every booking.** A contact is created or updated, the booking fields you choose are written to the contact, and your workflows can send confirmations and reminders.
- **Lets your agents book.** Addy can offer free times and book meetings in WhatsApp chats, and Ask AI can look up your bookings.

<Warning>
  Calendly webhooks need a **Calendly Standard plan or higher**. On the free plan, AdsEra can't receive bookings.
</Warning>

---

## Adding the integration

### Before you begin

- An AdsEra account with access to **Marketplace**.
- A Calendly Standard (or higher) plan.
- To receive bookings for your whole Calendly organization, a token from an organization **owner or admin**.

### Connect

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a token in Calendly">
  In Calendly, open **Integrations & apps → API & webhooks → Generate new token**. Copy the token.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste it in AdsEra">
  In AdsEra, open **Marketplace → Calendly** and click **Connect Calendly account**. Enter a **Label**, paste the **Calendly personal access token**, and choose **Bookings to receive**:
  - **My bookings**, or
  - **Everyone in my Calendly organization** (needs an owner or admin token).

  Click **Connect**.
</Step>
<Step title="Done">
  AdsEra checks the token and sets up the booking webhook in Calendly for you. The account appears with the status **active**.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Using the integration

The Calendly page has the same tabs as Cal.com: **Connections**, **Bookings**, **Used by agents** and **Activity**.

- **Contact fields:** click it on a connection to map a booking field or form question to a contact attribute. With no rows, bookings don't change contact attributes.
- **Addy:** open **Addy → Goals → Book appointments**, switch on **Offer bookings** and choose **Book in the chat (Calendly)**. Pick the account and event type, then save.
- **Used by agents:** choose what Addy, Ask AI and the Meta AI agent may do: offer free times, book, tell customers about their meetings, reschedule, see bookings, cancel. Anything that changes a booking starts **off**.

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Connecting fails with a plan or permission error">
  Calendly webhooks need the Standard plan or higher. If you chose **Everyone in my Calendly organization**, the token must come from an owner or admin.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The status shows &quot;error&quot;">
  The token was probably revoked. Click **Replace token** and paste a new token from the same Calendly account.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Bookings arrive but contact fields stay empty">
  Click **Contact fields** and map the booking questions to contact attributes, then click **Save**.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Removing the integration

1. Open **Marketplace → Calendly**.
2. Click **Disconnect** on the connection and confirm.

**What happens:**

- AdsEra removes its webhook from Calendly and deletes the connection: the token, signing key and account details.
- AdsEra stops receiving that account's bookings. Addy and Ask AI can no longer use it.
- **Bookings and contacts already in AdsEra are kept.**
- To use it again, connect it as new.
