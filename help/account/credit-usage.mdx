Addy, Closer and Ask AI use **AI credits**. Open **Billing → AI credits** to see your balance, buy more and check where they went.

## Your balance

- **Monthly credits** come with your plan. The page shows how many you've used and when they renew.
- **Top-up credits** are ones you buy. They **never expire** and are used after the monthly ones.

To buy more, choose **Top up** on the same page and enter how many credits you want. Every credit costs the same, ₹0.74, plus 18% GST.

## What credits buy

The page lists the typical credits per use, for example:

- a quick question or answer,
- Addy answering a customer on WhatsApp,
- creating a template or a broadcast,
- building or fixing a whole workflow,
- a deep plan (Plan mode in Ask AI),
- designing an image,
- Addy answering a phone call (per minute).

## Credit usage report

The **Credit usage** report has one row per WhatsApp chat, call, Ask AI chat or Closer run. Pick a **Month**, then open a row to see which message cost what. You can also reach the report from Ask AI's credits chip, through **Full usage report**.

## Limits

- **Closer:** set a monthly AI-credit limit in Closer's settings (0 means no limit).
- When credits run out, Addy and Ask AI tell you and link to a top-up.
