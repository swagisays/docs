AdsEra has three plans. All of them have **zero setup fees** and **no markup on Meta's WhatsApp charges**.

## Monthly or yearly

- **Monthly:** pay month to month.
- **Yearly:** pay for the year upfront and save **up to ~35%**, with **free dedicated onboarding**.

Prices are shown in **INR (₹)** or **USD ($)**.

</Note>
