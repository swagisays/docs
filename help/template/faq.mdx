<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why do I need a template at all?">
  WhatsApp only lets a business message someone first, or after 24 hours of silence, with a message Meta approved. That is a template. See [the 24-hour window](/help/template/rules-and-limits#the-24-hour-window).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
  Usually a few minutes, at most 24 hours. The status changes by itself; click **Sync Templates** to check.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Which category should I pick?">
  Offers, launches, greetings: **Marketing**. Updates about something the person asked for (visit confirmed, payment due): **Utility**. Login codes: **Authentication**. When unsure, Marketing.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I change the category later?">
  No. Make a new template with the right category.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I put the customer’s name in the message?">
  Click **Add Variable** in the message and name it, like `{{name}}`, give a sample, and fill it from the contact’s first name when you send. See [Variables and samples](/help/template/variables).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I send photos of 3 flats in one message?">
  Yes, with a [carousel](/help/template/carousel): 2 to 10 cards with a photo and text each.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I make a catalog or product template?">
  Not in AdsEra. To show products in a chat, use a [quick reply with a product list](/help/snippet/interactive).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I copy a template?">
  Not yet. Click **New template** and paste the text from the old one.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why was my template rejected?">
  The card says why and how to fix it. See [Statuses and rejections](/help/template/status).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My template says Paused. What now?">
  People blocked or reported it. Improve the wording and send only to people who expect it. See [Statuses](/help/template/status).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I use a template in English and Hindi?">
  Make two templates with the same name, one per language.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I made a template in Meta’s WhatsApp Manager. How do I see it here?">
  Click **Sync Templates**.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
