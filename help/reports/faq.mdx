<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did “Your cards” on Home go?">
    They moved to **Reports** on the icon rail. Each view you had is now a report with the same cards, and old bookmarks to a view open the matching report. Home shows your first three reports under **Your reports**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many reports and cards can I have?">
    As many reports as you like, up to 16 cards each.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see my reports?">
    Only you, until you **Share** one. A shared report can be read by your whole team; only you can change it. See [Sharing and organising](/help/reports/sharing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will my team see data they shouldn’t on a shared report?">
    No. Every card is checked against the reader’s own role: they see only the areas and records their role allows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can’t I make a report?">
    Your role can only read reports. An admin can tick **Home & reports → Make and change reports** for your role in **Settings → Team members**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a card I want not in the list?">
    You only see cards for areas your role can open. Ads cards appear once an ad account is connected under **Ads → Meta Ads**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do report dates change my cards’ settings?">
    No. Pick **Each card’s own dates** to go back to each card’s own time range. See [Filters and dates](/help/reports/filters).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download a whole report?">
    Not as one file. Each table card downloads its rows as CSV or Excel. See [Tables](/help/reports/tables).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can reports be emailed to me every week?">
    Not yet. Open the report, or leave it on a screen with **Auto-refresh**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I start again?">
    **Edit layout → Reset all reports to the starting layout** puts back Sales, WhatsApp and Plan & billing and removes your own reports. It asks first and can’t be undone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
