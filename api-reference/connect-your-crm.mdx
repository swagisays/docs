---
title: "Use AdsEra with your own CRM"
description: "Keep working in your CRM. AdsEra runs WhatsApp, the AI agent and calls, and sends everything to you."
---

AdsEra can run WhatsApp, the AI chat agent, AI calls and follow-ups in the background while your team works in your own CRM. Setup has three parts:

1. **Get your API keys.** You need them to read data and send messages.
2. **Add a webhook.** AdsEra posts each message and call to your CRM as it happens.
3. **Read history when you need it.** Use this to load a full conversation or old calls.

You don't need to change anything inside AdsEra for this. The AI keeps answering, and your CRM shows what it said.

## 1. Get your API keys

In AdsEra, open [**Settings → API Keys**](https://app.adsera.in/setting/api-keys) and create a key. You get a **Key** and a **Secret**. Send both headers with every request:

```http
x-api-key: YOUR_APP_ID
x-api-secret: YOUR_APP_SECRET
```

All URLs start with `https://app.adsera.in`.

## 2. Add a webhook

Open [**Settings → Webhooks**](https://app.adsera.in/setting/webhooks) and add your CRM's URL. Then:

- **Pick your WhatsApp numbers.**
- **Tick these events:**

| Event | When it arrives | Use it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `message.new` | A customer sends a message | Show the incoming message, create a lead |
| `message.sent` | AdsEra sends a message: the AI, your team, a template, a campaign, a follow-up | Show what was sent and who sent it |
| `message.status_updated` | A message is delivered, read or fails | Show ticks |
| `call.ended` | A WhatsApp call ends, or is missed or rejected | Log the call |
| `call.summary` | The AI has summarised a call it took | Add the summary to the call log |
| `addy.lead_updated` | Addy, the AI agent, moves a lead to a new temperature or stage (for example warm → hot) | Create or update the lead with its score and summary |
| `addy.handoff` | Addy hands a chat to a person | Alert the salesperson, with Addy's summary |
| `contact.created` / `contact.updated` | A contact is added or changed in AdsEra (by hand, an import, a form). New WhatsApp chats come as `message.new`. | Keep contacts in sync |

Every webhook is a `POST` with this body:

```json
{
  "event": "message.sent",
  "timestamp": "2026-11-20T10:15:02.000Z",
  "data": { "...": "the message, call or contact" }
}
```

Each message has a ready-to-show `preview` (the text, or a template with its values filled in) and a `sentBy` (`Customer`, `AI`, `Team`, `Broadcast (bulk campaign)` or `Automation (sequence)`). You don't need to decode WhatsApp message types. See [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks/overview) for every field.

<Tip>
Answer with any `2xx` within 5 seconds. Do slow work after you answer. Failed deliveries are not retried, so use the read endpoints below to fill gaps. [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks/overview) covers security, sample code in Node.js, Python and PHP, and troubleshooting.
</Tip>

## 3. Read history

| What | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| A contact's whole WhatsApp conversation | [`GET /api/pub/wa/messages?phone=…`](/api-reference/msg/conversation) |
| Calls, with summary and recording | [`GET /api/pub/wa/call/list`](/api-reference/call/list), [`GET /api/pub/wa/call/{id}`](/api-reference/call/get) |
| The call's audio | [`GET /api/pub/wa/call/{id}/recording`](/api-reference/call/recording) |
| A contact, with the AI's summary of the lead | [`GET /api/pub/crm/contacts/lookup?phone=…`](/api-reference/crm/contacts/lookup) |

```bash
curl "https://app.adsera.in/api/pub/wa/messages?phone=919876543210" \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_APP_ID" -H "x-api-secret: YOUR_APP_SECRET"
```

## Work with the AI agent (Addy)

From your CRM you can also see what Addy learned about a customer, take a chat over while your salesperson replies, hand it back, and tell Addy when an order ships. See the [Addy API](/api-reference/addy/overview).

## Send from your CRM (optional)

When your team replies from your CRM, send it through AdsEra so it goes out on your WhatsApp number:

- **Inside 24 hours of the customer's last message:** [Send Message](/api-reference/msg/msg), any text.
- **Otherwise:** [Send Template](/api-reference/msg/send-v2), an approved template.

When you create leads in your CRM, push them to AdsEra with [Create or Update a Contact](/api-reference/crm/contacts/upsert) so the AI and follow-ups can work them.
